# Primary-source check of the proposal bibliography, 2026-10-16

These checks identify support for specific claims, not an exhaustive novelty search or a reproduction of any published result.

- [SyncRA, original paper §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34363v1): the linked paper is titled *Learning Temporal Correspondence in Omni-Modal Models*. It trains an intermediate within-video synchrony contrastive objective together with answer supervision; the projection head is removed at inference. It is not the static mean-activation inference method described in the original v7 proposal. Its introduction explicitly distinguishes retained temporal information from poor cross-modal correspondence. This supports testing alignment supervision but does not establish the proposal's universal claim that attention reweighting cannot carry order because V is not RoPE-rotated.
- [AVTrace, original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19991v1): a silver-standard diagnostic suite for grounding, synchronization, chain parsing and related temporal skills. Its abstract warns against treating semantic reference overlap as localization. The cited source does not supply the stated theorem that event binding requires counterfactual interaction masking. Its benchmark evidence is relevant to evaluation design, not a proof that JEP-CLC is necessary.
- [TimeLens §5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.14698v2): evaluates timestamp encodings and training/data recipes for video temporal grounding, including interleaved textual timestamps. This is not the claimed proof of position-blind Value states or necessity of hidden-state contrast. [TimeLens2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17423v1) studies generalist single/multi-interval grounding with a unified output interface; its introduction emphasizes varied durations, viewpoints, repeated evidence and query forms. These papers reinforce the need for clean temporal annotations and broad tasks.
- [RED §3 and AppendixA.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02976v1): frozen-model decoding using question-guided evidence-type selection and PMI contributions relative to a question-only reference. It does include an evidence-type selection step; it is not direct evidence that subtracting two internal states automatically purifies event/order directions. Its formulation emphasizes that the interpretation of a contrast depends on the reference construction.
- [AHA §4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24052v2): preference-data construction with counterfactual hard negatives and DPO post-training, including human validation. It is not an inference-only latent state contrast method. Its success cannot be transferred as a guarantee that fixed-weight JEP-CLC will work.
- [Meerkat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01851): already relevant prior work on audio-visual grounding; the earlier v6 notes link the official ECCV paper for local optimal transport and cross-attention supervision. OT plus attention is insufficient by itself to establish novelty.

Recommended change to Yoshua's rationale: cite these methods accurately and state the causal/architectural explanations as hypotheses until tested. A finite set of unsuccessful interventions does not falsify every attention-only method, every learned adapter, or every unconstrained media-to-query mapping.
