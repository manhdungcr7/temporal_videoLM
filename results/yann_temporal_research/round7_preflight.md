# R7 — bounded retrieval and semantic question representation

R6 grounding-only validation endpoint CE is6.16 despite best checkpoint selection; diagnostic answer choices21/48, while oracle stays42/48. Learning retrieval on31 recordings can badly overfit. R4 warm-start validation CE8.78 is even worse than a typical uniform-bank CE; raw dot products have unrestricted norm and can be confidently wrong. Do not conclude that more training alone will solve this.

R7 keeps the oracle-trained reader fixed, the same R4 retrieval warm start, the same1600-step grounding-only objective and recording-averaged endpoint-CE selection. Replace raw query/key dot product by normalized cosine times fixed8 on both servers. Server1 keeps native media-conditioned last-prompt query. Server2 uses the mean of native block18 states of the literal question tokens from a separate text-only forward (no media/options/answers). This is a semantic-query representation diagnostic with a shared bounded scorer, not an isolated claim that cosine or text-only encoding is new. The text-only question state may lack the audio-derived meaning of an implicit reference such as 'the narrated event'; even successful explicit-label localization would not establish transfer to those queries.

No query parsing, fixed audio-first rule, task-name router, answer keywords or benchmark training are added. Reading native text representations, cosine retrieval and stagewise distillation are established components; novelty would still need a stronger general AV mechanism and independent causal-use evidence.
